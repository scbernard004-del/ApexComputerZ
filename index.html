Common questions

Are the laptops new or refurbished?

Condition varies by listing. Ask Apex Computers for the condition, battery health, keyboard layout and photos of the exact unit before you pay. A product render does not establish the unit’s condition.

How do I place an order?

Tap Buy on a product to open WhatsApp with its details, or tap Enquire to ask a question. We confirm availability, price and payment with you. No payment is taken on this website.

Can I arrange delivery?

Contact Apex Computers with your location to agree delivery availability, cost and timing before payment. No delivery charge is included in the catalogue prices.

What about warranty and returns?

Ask for the warranty and return terms for the specific unit in writing before payment. These are confirmed directly with Apex Computers.

What happens to my enquiry details?

Your name, contact details, chosen products and message are used to answer your enquiry. They are emailed to Apex Computers. Favourites and your enquiry bag stay in this browser; there are no advertising trackers.
